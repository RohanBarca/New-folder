# 🏥 MedSync

### AI-Powered Pre-Consultation Healthcare Assistant

MedSync is an AI-assisted healthcare platform designed to streamline the process that happens **before a patient meets a doctor**.

Patients often have medical information scattered across prescriptions, reports, OPD records, and previous consultations. Doctors then have to spend valuable consultation time manually collecting and organizing this information.

MedSync brings this information together, processes medical documents, assists patients with conversational history-taking, and presents structured information to healthcare professionals.

> **MedSync does not replace doctors. It assists doctors by reducing repetitive information-gathering and organizing patient information before consultation.**

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Healthcare consultations often face several challenges:

- Patient medical records are fragmented across different documents and hospitals.
- Doctors spend significant time manually reviewing old reports and prescriptions.
- Patients may forget important details about their medical history.
- Traditional forms can be difficult for patients to complete accurately.
- Language barriers can affect communication between patients and healthcare professionals.
- Important information may be missed during a short consultation.
- Ayurvedic history-taking requires additional patient information that may not be captured by conventional forms.

MedSync addresses these challenges through an AI-assisted pre-consultation workflow.
